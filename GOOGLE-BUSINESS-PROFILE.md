# Google Business Profile checklist

1. Go to [Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/) while signed into the Google account that should own the business listing.
2. Search “Ebube Israel Furniture Enterprise”. If it already appears, choose **Claim this business**; otherwise choose **Add your business to Google**.
3. Use the exact business name consistently: **Ebube Israel Furniture Enterprise**. Choose the closest category, such as **Furniture store** or **Furniture maker**.
4. Add the precise street address. If customers do not visit the workshop, choose a service area instead and include Agbor and the relevant delivery towns.
5. Add both phone numbers, business hours, photos of finished work, and the TikTok/Instagram links where available.
6. Once this website is public, add its full `https://` address to the Website field.
7. Complete Google’s verification. After it is live, ask satisfied customers for honest Google reviews and reply to each one.

Keep the business name, phone number, address and website identical on Google, the website, Facebook and Instagram. This consistency helps local search visibility.
