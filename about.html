About Ebube Israel

Comfort, character
and craft.

Ebube Israel Furniture Enterprise is a growing furniture and interior business serving Agbor, Delta State and beyond.

We believe the best furniture does more than fill a room. It supports daily life, reflects your taste and gives your home a sense of ease.

Our work spans custom sofas, beds, dining pieces, fitted kitchens, wardrobes, media walls and complete interior finishing. We approach each request with care—whether it starts with a detailed plan or a simple inspiration photo.
